Broke-ish privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Broke-ish is a budgeting app that shows you how much you can safely spend each day. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What the app stores

Everything you enter, including balances, bills, income, spending, goals and debts, is saved only in the app’s storage on your own device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Broke-ish has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your budget is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Backups

If you choose Export a backup, the app creates a file on your device. Where that file goes after that, such as your email or cloud storage, is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Erase everything, inside the app, or uninstall the app. Either one removes your data from the device.

Children

Broke-ish is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
